- **NICE/OGL:** NICE guidance is published under the Open Government Licence v3.0 per NICE's notice of rights — same basis as the 5 NICE docs already in the corpus. Currency of each guideline re-confirmed on nice.org.uk (NG51: 2024 update; NG148: 2023 update; CG32: 2017 surveillance, no update needed, still live).
- **Critical Care (BMC/Springer):** all research articles Open Access CC BY 4.0 — verified verbatim on the delirium meta-review article page (Rights and permissions section); applied journal-wide to the ventilation IPD meta-analysis (same journal, same policy).
- **PLOS ONE:** all research articles CC BY per journal policy (applies to both PLOS ONE docs).
